Slide 2 / 10 — Problem

😰 The Problem

Micro-entrepreneurs operate solo — overwhelmed by front-of-house duties, with no time for back-office management.

⏰
No time to record daily income and expenses
📊
No ability to create financial summaries or track profitability
🔍
No data-driven insights to guide business decisions
📝
Manual bookkeeping is time-consuming and error-prone
[source: participant]
Slide 3 / 10 — Solution

💡 The Solution — 3-Phase Roadmap

P0 — MVP
🤖 AI Slip Scanning + Auto Record
Photograph slip/order → AI extracts data via OCR → auto-save to database
P1 — Phase 2
📊 Dashboard & Daily Summary
Income/expense, daily/weekly/monthly profit as visual dashboard
P2 — Phase 3
💡 AI Business Recommendations
Analyze accumulated data → actionable suggestions (best-sellers, peak hours, cost reduction)
[source: participant]
